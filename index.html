What is stick-out?

 
Stick-out is how far the tool reaches out of its holder: measure from the face of the holder (where the bar or drill comes out of the clamp, collet or sleeve) to the cutting tip of the insert – or to the point of the drill / tap.

 
    
  
  • Measure along the tool, parallel to Z, with a scale or calipers.
    • 
  
  • Bar Ø is the shank diameter of the boring bar (the round part), not the insert.
    • 
  
  • Meerplot uses it to warn you when the holder would hit the part – a bore deeper than the stick-out means the holder crashes into the face.
    • 
  
  • Rule of thumb for chatter: steel bars up to about 4 × Bar Ø, carbide bars up to about 6–8 × Bar Ø.
    •

How to use G-Code Backplot

 
    
  
  • Load a program: click 📂 Open, paste into the editor on the left, or try one of the Examples.
    • 
  
  • See the toolpath: the graph on the right updates as you type. Orange dashed lines are rapid moves (G0); solid lines are cutting moves.
    • 
  
  • Play it: press ▶ Play (or Space). Use Speed to go faster or slower than the real machine.
    • 
  
  • Find a line: click a point on the graph to jump to its line, or click a line in the editor to highlight its move on the graph.
    • 
  
  • Set up your machine: open ⚙ Stock & Machine to enter the bar size, chuck and travel limits. All sizes use the program's units (inch for G20, mm for G21).
    • 
  
  • Several programs: each program opens in its own tab (top left). Click ＋ for a new one, type the file name in the 📄 box, and double-click a tab to rename it. Each tab keeps its own stock, tools and units.
    • 
  
  • Radius / chamfer: in 🧰 Shop Tools → Radius / Chamfer, give the start point and the size; it writes the G2/G3 or chamfer moves for you.
    • 
  
  • Blueprint: in 🧰 Shop Tools, click 📐 Blueprint to open a PDF or picture of the drawing. Type (or click) the diameters, lengths, chamfers and radii; the finished part is drawn on the graph and you can insert a G71/G70 program for it.
    • 
  
  • Two-sided parts: when the program reaches a comment like (TURN ROLL AROUND) or (FLIP PART), the part is flipped end for end on the graph and the program carries on on the other end.
    • 
  
  • 📂 Macros: saved G-code in folders. Pick one and press ⤓ Insert below cursor (or double-click it). Subprograms can be ⤵ called – the M98 line goes at the cursor and the subprogram is added at the end. Select lines and press + Macro to save your own.
    • 
  
  • Jump the simulation to a line: triple-click the line in the code.
    • 
  
  • Find (Ctrl+F): searches only the G-code. Enter = next, Shift+Enter = previous, Esc closes. Tick “Code only” to skip comments.
    • 
  
  • Quick find: ⇣ Tool, ⇣ Feed and ⇣ Speed jump to the next tool change, feed or speed (Shift+click goes back, or Alt+T / Alt+F / Alt+S).
    • 
  
  • Bookmarks: 🔖 marks the cursor line (Ctrl+F2), ◂ ▸ jump between marks (Shift+F2 / F2), ☰ lists them.
    • 
  
  • Measure: press 📏 Measure on the graph, then click two points – you get ΔX, ΔZ, the distance and the angle.
    • 
  
  • Renumber, Shift / Mirror: in the ✎ Format menu. Press ↶ Undo if a change isn’t what you wanted.
    • 
  
  • Program Limits and Setup Sheet: in 🧰 Shop Tools. The setup sheet can be printed or downloaded for the operator.
    • 
  
  • Edges not broken: every 90° outside corner should get a chamfer or radius. Sharp ones are listed under Problems as “Edge not broken” – click it to jump to the line, then use ◜ Radius / Chamfer.
    • 
  
  • Steady rest: sits 5 in back from the Z0 face. It closes on (CLOSE STEADY REST) and opens on (OPEN STEADY REST); while closed, the graph shows it touching the part and warns if a tool runs into it. Change the distance, or set it to always closed, in Stock & machine.
    • 
  
  • Boring bars: in 🔧 Tool List set a tool's type to Boring, then enter the bar diameter, stick-out and whether it runs upside down. The bar is drawn in the hole and checked for hitting the wall. Set the stock's hole size in Stock & Machine.
    • 
  
  • Check for problems: crashes and mistakes are listed under Problems below the editor. Click one to jump to the line.
    •
